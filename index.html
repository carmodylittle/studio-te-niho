About

Carmody Little creates under the name Te Niho, a subtle nod to his Māori ancestry. In Te Reo Māori (the native language of New Zealand / Aotearoa), Te Niho translates to "the tooth" or "teeth" a moniker that perfectly reflects the sharp, signature style of his work.

Founded in Berlin, Carmody’s pieces meet at the intersection of the sculptural and the functional. His work delivers beautifully elegant designs that carry a distinct punk edge, serving as a direct love letter to the raw energy of Berlin itself.
